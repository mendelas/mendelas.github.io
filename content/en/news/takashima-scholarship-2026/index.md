I have been selected as a scholarship student of the **Takashima Science and Technology Promotion Foundation** (公益財団法人 高島科学技術振興財団). The scholarship runs for three years, from 2026 through 2029.

The foundation supports students conducting research in science and technology, allowing them to concentrate on their research activities. I will use this support for my doctoral work on Physical AI, SLAM, and Integrated Sensing and Communication (ISAC).

I am deeply grateful to the Takashima Science and Technology Promotion Foundation for their support.
