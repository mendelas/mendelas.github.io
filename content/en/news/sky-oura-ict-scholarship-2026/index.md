I have been selected as a scholarship student of the **Sky Oura ICT Scholarship Foundation** (公益財団法人 Sky大浦ICT奨学財団). The scholarship runs from April 2026 through March 2029, covering the standard three-year term of my doctoral program.

The foundation provides non-repayable scholarships to students who aspire to build careers in the ICT field, allowing them to concentrate on their studies and research. I will use this support for my doctoral work on Physical AI, SLAM, and Integrated Sensing and Communication (ISAC).

I am deeply grateful to the Sky Oura ICT Scholarship Foundation for their support.
